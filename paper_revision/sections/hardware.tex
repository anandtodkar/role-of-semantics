\section{Hardware-Based Evaluation Design}
\label{sec:hardware}
\subsection{Controller acquisition and physical validation boundary}
IIH 2.6.1 on an Industrial Edge IPC427E collects the CPU 1518-4 PN/DP
test-variable model (author-confirmed, 2026-10-08). Source/aggregate exports and
mapping audit are unavailable. R4 is offline; no sensor/actuator trial logs
exist. The scenarios below are protocol, not results.

The recommended rig is two guarded 24~V handling stations, optionally with a
low-head instrumented water-fill module. Use calibrated sensors and contain
fluid; select equipment and disturbances through a competent engineer's risk
assessment. Do not add pressure or heat hazards merely to mimic the benchmark.

The S7-1500 retains sequencing, live guards, output authority and acknowledgement;
IPC427E hosts acquisition and may host validation after resource checks. IPC227G
may support replay. Record CPU/firmware, TIA, I/O, OPC UA licensing and IPC
resources. Model inference may be remote. Do not presume standard CPUs are
safety-rated.

\subsection{Command interface and current-state protection}
Expose allowlisted supervisory tools, not raw outputs or unrestricted PLC access.
The PLC mailbox carries a unique request, target, native-unit value, approval
and expiry/state reference. Use a staged sequence/commit handshake, then have
the PLC recheck current guards and record its decision/acknowledgement. Commission
duplicates, timeouts and reconnects without an LLM. Stale or bad-quality evidence
means refresh or abstain. PLC guards and independent protection remain authoritative;
the agent is outside hard-real-time control.

\subsection{Trial sequence and matched comparisons}
Commission manually first; freeze tasks, labels and episode splits. Compare
matched baselines in read-only shadow mode; never send known-invalid actions.
Enable only approved bounded legal proposals under supervision. Hold out a
station/configuration to measure transfer and record mappings, code changes,
regressions and engineering time. Replay unsafe disturbances and label them as
replay, not physical incidents.

\subsection{Measurements, incident diagnosis and uncertainty}
Measure grounded answers/evidence, invalid proposals admitted, legal proposals
rejected, task completion and physical outcome separately. Distinguish agent,
gateway and PLC decisions. Record retrieval/validation/dispatch/ack latency,
p50/p95, deadline misses, observation quality/age and clock uncertainty. Set
freshness thresholds for the workload, not from the synthetic benchmark.

For diagnosis, preregister candidate causes and multi-cause labels. A graph path
is not causal proof; use MAP@3 only for a matched ranked-cause task. CausalTrace
comparison requires its source and artefact.

Split by asset/episode, and account for within-episode dependence. Set sample
sizes from pilot variability and engineer-agreed thresholds. Zero events in 300
independent trials still permits an approximate 1\% upper 95\% risk bound; it is
not a safety claim. Record provenance and separate physical, hardware-in-loop
and replay traces using the supplied schema.
